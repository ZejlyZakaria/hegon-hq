## Narrow correction

A slot is removed only when all possible live subjects are proven invalid; absence of canonical ownership retains the existing legacy scope resolver. NULL clears are not treated as surviving opinions that justify a projection.

The lineage pass still appends independent NULL CORRECTION successors to invalid stable scope_keys and retains their original assertions. Removed its redundant ordinal/score-based projection deletion entirely.
